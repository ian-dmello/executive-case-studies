# Executive Summary

I was seconded to the Internal Audit function of a leading Middle Eastern telecommunications group, where I supported two complementary engagements.

The first assignment focused on the parent telecommunications group and centred on enterprise strategy, value creation, cost optimisation and strategic transaction reviews. The work included benchmarking cost optimisation initiatives, evaluating enterprise-wide value creation programmes, reviewing strategic recommendations prepared by external advisors and assessing risks associated with a significant acquisition.

The second assignment focused on the Group's digital technology subsidiary, which delivers Internet of Things (IoT) solutions to support smart cities, smart manufacturing, smart logistics and digital transformation initiatives. My responsibilities included strengthening governance over financial reporting, developing enterprise risk registers, evaluating cyber security governance and improving risk management across product development, marketing and after-sales operations.

Together, these engagements provided exposure to both strategic decision-making at Group level and governance, risk and operational excellence within a rapidly expanding technology business.

# Business Context

The telecommunications group was pursuing growth through strategic investments, operational transformation and enterprise-wide value creation initiatives while simultaneously expanding its digital capabilities through its specialised technology subsidiary.

Executive management required independent assurance and strategic insights across two distinct but connected areas:

## Group Level

- Strategy execution
- Cost optimisation
- Value creation

- Financial performance
- Benchmarking against industry peers

## Digital Technology Subsidiary

The subsidiary focused on accelerating digital transformation by delivering Internet of Things (IoT) solutions across smart cities, smart manufacturing and smart logistics ecosystems.

Management required stronger governance over:

- Financial reporting
- Enterprise Risk Management (ERM)
- Cyber Security Governance
- Product Development
- Marketing Operations
- After-sales Support
- Technology Risk
