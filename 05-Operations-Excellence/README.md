# Operations Excellence

## Overview

This practice brings together case studies focused on **operational improvement, process effectiveness, operating models, organisational performance and sustainable business execution**.

The case studies demonstrate how operational issues can be assessed systematically and translated into practical improvement initiatives.

## Cross-Practice Perspective

Operational excellence is closely connected with strategy, finance, governance and transformation.

Cases in this practice may therefore also intersect with:

- **Strategy & Transformation** – translating strategic objectives into operating-model and execution improvements
- **Finance & Performance** – improving efficiency, cost management and financial performance
- **Risk, Governance & Internal Audit** – strengthening process controls and operational risk management
- **Deals & Investment** – assessing operational feasibility and execution requirements

Where a case has significant relevance to another practice, the case remains in its **primary practice folder** and is cross-referenced rather than duplicated.

## Approach

The operational-excellence perspective generally considers:

**Current State → Process Assessment → Root Causes → Improvement Opportunities → Target Operating Model → Implementation → Performance Monitoring**

The specific methodology varies by case and is described within the individual case study.

---

## Professional Perspective

Operational excellence is not simply about reducing costs.

Sustainable improvement requires understanding:

- How the business operates
- Where processes create value
- Where inefficiencies occur
- Why performance gaps exist
- Which controls are necessary
- How technology can support improvement
- How responsibilities and accountability should be structured
- How improvements can be measured and sustained

My approach is therefore to connect:

**Process → People → Technology → Controls → Performance → Business Outcomes**
