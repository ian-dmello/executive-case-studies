# Consulting Practice Growth & Operations for Protiviti Qatar

**Organisation:** Protiviti Qatar  
**Role:** Manager – Internal Audit & Business Advisory Services  
**Duration:** August 2014 – January 2016

---

# Executive Summary

When I joined Protiviti Qatar in August 2014, the Internal Audit and Business Advisory practice was undergoing a period of rebuilding. Beyond leading major consulting engagements, I assumed responsibilities that extended across the entire consulting lifecycle, including business development, proposal management, engagement delivery, resource planning and commercial operations.

The objective was not only to execute high-quality consulting assignments but also to strengthen the practice's ability to win new business, deliver projects efficiently and improve client satisfaction.

During my tenure, I contributed to the preparation of approximately **15–20 technical and commercial proposals** across governance, internal audit, enterprise risk management, business transformation, policy development and project assurance services.

In parallel, I managed or supported the delivery of several of the firm's largest consulting engagements, coordinated resource deployment across multiple projects, supported commercial billing and utilisation management, and worked closely with Partners and Directors to expand the practice.

The combination of successful proposal development, high-quality delivery and effective operational management contributed to the continued growth and reputation of Protiviti Qatar's consulting practice.
