# Corporate Governance & SOX Transformation
## Vedanta Group (Sterlite Industries, Hindustan Zinc, BALCO & Sterlite Energy)
### KPMG India | 2007–2010

---

## Executive Summary

Led multiple governance and SOX Section 404 engagements across the Vedanta Group while at KPMG India. The assignments focused on strengthening internal controls, improving corporate governance, and enhancing Board oversight across several listed and operating subsidiaries. The engagement combined regulatory compliance with governance transformation by developing policies, governance frameworks, and remediation strategies that were formally adopted by management and the Board of Directors.

---

## Client

- Diversified Mining, Metals & Energy Conglomerate
- Multiple Listed & Operating Subsidiaries
- Locations across India

---

## Business Challenge

The Group operated through multiple subsidiaries with varying business processes, governance practices, and internal control maturity. Maintaining a consistent SOX-compliant control environment while strengthening corporate governance across the enterprise required standardized frameworks, executive alignment, and Board-level oversight.

---

## Objectives

- Strengthen SOX Section 404 compliance
- Standardize internal control practices
- Enhance Board governance
- Develop sustainable governance documentation
- Reduce recurring control deficiencies
- Improve executive accountability

---

## Consulting Approach

### SOX Compliance

- Led SOX implementation across multiple Vedanta Group entities.
- Managed project teams across seven operating locations.
- Conducted walkthroughs and process documentation.
- Prepared Risk & Control Matrices (RCMs).
- Evaluated Design and Operating Effectiveness of Controls.
- Performed Entity Level Assessments (ELA).
- Conducted Segregation of Duties (SoD) analysis.
- Identified control deficiencies and remediation opportunities.
- Facilitated discussions with Finance, Treasury and Company Secretarial leadership.

### Corporate Governance Transformation

Designed governance documentation including:

- Board Charter
- Audit Committee Charter
- Committee Charters
- Risk Management Policy
- Compliance Framework
- Corporate Governance Policy
- Annual Report Governance Disclosure Templates
- Accounting Policy Disclosure Templates

Prepared an Executive Summary of governance recommendations which was presented to and adopted by the Board of Directors.
