**Client:** Indian High Commission, London (Academic International Immersion)

## Executive Summary

As part of the International Immersion Programme at the Indian Institute of Management Ahmedabad (IIMA), our three-member team was selected to prepare a strategic research report for the **Indian High Commission, London** on the proposed **UK–India Green Growth Equity Fund (GGEF)**.

The assignment supported the establishment of the Green Growth Equity Fund, a joint initiative of the Governments of India and the United Kingdom designed to increase investment in renewable energy and sustainable infrastructure projects across India.

Within the team, I led a significant portion of the research and drafting effort, conducting approximately **100 hours of research** using government publications, infrastructure policy papers, investment reports and research published by leading global consulting firms.

The completed report addressed three strategic questions posed by the High Commission regarding infrastructure investment, renewable energy and improving the value proposition of the proposed fund. The report and supporting research material were formally accepted by officials at the Indian High Commission.

---

# Business Context

India's infrastructure financing requirements are among the largest in the world. To support long-term investment into renewable energy and sustainable infrastructure, the Governments of India and the United Kingdom proposed the **UK–India Green Growth Equity Fund**, operating under the National Investment and Infrastructure Fund (NIIF).

The objective was to create a commercially viable investment platform capable of attracting long-term institutional capital into renewable energy, clean transportation, water treatment and other environmentally sustainable infrastructure projects.

The Indian High Commission sought an independent assessment addressing:

- Whether India's infrastructure sector represented an attractive investment destination for UK and global investors.
- Whether the proposed Green Growth Equity Fund offered a compelling value proposition.
- What policy initiatives could further improve investor confidence and fund attractiveness.

# Key Recommendations

## Renewable Energy

Recommended:

- Long-term tariff certainty.
- Expansion of Renewable Energy Certificates.
- Improved market access.
- Greater adoption of captive renewable projects.
- Development of renewable energy parks.

---

## Infrastructure

Recommended:

- Improving land acquisition processes.
- Stronger contractual enforcement.
- More predictable regulatory frameworks.
- Better project governance.
- Enhanced political and regulatory risk mitigation.
- Improved long-term infrastructure planning.
