# Executive Summary

As Group Internal Audit Manager, I was responsible for leading governance, risk and advisory initiatives across a diversified business group comprising eighteen subsidiaries.

While my primary responsibility was managing the annual Internal Audit programme, my role evolved significantly beyond traditional assurance activities. I worked closely with executive management to strengthen governance, improve enterprise risk visibility, investigate significant frauds, enhance management reporting and promote a stronger internal control culture across the Group.

Over the engagement, I successfully managed the delivery of **46 risk-based internal audits** while also leading several strategic initiatives that strengthened governance capability across the organisation.

# Business Context

The Group operated across multiple industries including automotive, construction, industrial services, retail, facilities management and trading.

Rapid business growth created increasing demands for stronger governance, better visibility over enterprise risks and more consistent control practices across subsidiaries.

Executive management expected Internal Audit to evolve from a compliance-focused function into a trusted business partner capable of providing strategic insights and practical recommendations.

---

# My Responsibilities

My responsibilities included:

- Managing the annual risk-based Internal Audit programme.
- Delivering **46 annual audits** across multiple subsidiaries.
- Leading special investigations and fraud reviews.
- Preparing audit plans, audit programmes and enterprise risk registers.
- Developing monthly executive MIS reports.
- Publishing monthly Audit Alerts for knowledge sharing.
- Delivering Internal Control training for General Managers and senior leadership teams.
- Supporting Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) implementation.
- Preparing presentations supporting Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) initiatives.
- Reporting significant observations to executive management and the Audit Committee.

---

# Strategic Initiatives

## Enterprise Audit Programme

Managed the execution of the annual Internal Audit Plan across eighteen subsidiaries while ensuring timely completion, quality reporting and consistent application of risk-based audit methodologies.

The programme covered operational, financial, compliance and strategic risks across diverse industries.

---

## Fraud Investigations

Led sensitive investigations involving employee misconduct, management fraud allegations and other confidential assignments requested by executive leadership.

These engagements required independent investigation, evidence gathering, root cause analysis and practical recommendations to strengthen governance while maintaining confidentiality.

---

## Enterprise Control Culture

Designed and delivered Internal Control awareness programmes for senior management across all subsidiaries.

The objective was to improve management ownership of controls, strengthen governance awareness and embed better risk management practices throughout the organisation.

---

## Knowledge Management

Established a monthly **Audit Alert** publication highlighting:

- Emerging business risks
- Governance best practices
- Common audit observations
- Regulatory developments
- Practical control improvements

The initiative promoted knowledge sharing and improved governance consistency across the Group.

---

## Executive Reporting

Developed monthly Management Information System (MIS) reports summarising:

- Audit progress
- Significant observations
- High-risk issues
- Management action plans
- Emerging risks

The reporting framework enabled executives to monitor governance performance through concise, decision-focused dashboards.

---

## Governance Frameworks

Prepared:

- Risk Registers
- Audit Programmes
- Strategy Updates
- Knowledge Transfer Documents

These standardised methodologies improved audit quality, consistency and knowledge sharing within the Internal Audit function.

---

## Enterprise Risk Management & ESG

Supported the implementation of Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) and Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) initiatives through executive presentations, governance frameworks and implementation guidance.
