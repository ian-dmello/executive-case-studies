# Enterprise Risk Management Framework Design
## Binani Cement Limited
### KPMG India | 2009–2010

---

## Executive Summary

Led the design and implementation of an Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) framework for Binani Cement to support Clause 49 risk governance requirements. The engagement established enterprise-wide risk governance, strengthened Board oversight, and introduced structured Control Self-Assessments (CSA) to enhance executive accountability and improve risk-informed decision making.

---

## Client

- Integrated Cement Manufacturer
- Listed Indian Corporate

---

## Business Challenge

The organization required a structured Enterprise Risk Management framework to identify, assess, monitor, and report enterprise risks while strengthening governance in line with Clause 49 requirements. Existing risk management practices lacked consistency and comprehensive Board reporting.

---

## Objectives

- Design an Enterprise Risk Management Framework.
- Develop a centralized Risk Register.
- Strengthen Board and Audit Committee oversight.
- Support CEO/CFO certification requirements.
- Develop Internal Control Frameworks.
- Formalize Risk Governance policies.

---

## Consulting Approach

### Enterprise Risk Assessment

- Facilitated enterprise risk workshops with senior management.
- Identified strategic, operational, financial and compliance risks.
- Prioritized risks using standardized impact and likelihood criteria.
- Assigned risk ownership across business functions.

### Governance Framework

Developed:

- Enterprise Risk Management Policy
- Internal Control Framework Policy
- Enterprise Risk Register
- Risk Heat Maps
- Control Self-Assessment (CSA) Framework
- CEO/CFO Certification Checklists

### Board Reporting

Prepared executive presentations for the Audit Committee highlighting:

- Key Enterprise Risks
- Risk Prioritization
- Control Gaps
- Mitigation Plans
- Governance Recommendations
