# Deals & Investment

## Overview

This practice brings together case studies involving **M&A, investment evaluation, feasibility assessment, strategic investments and growth-oriented capital allocation**.

The case studies demonstrate how strategic, financial, operational and risk considerations can be brought together to evaluate investment opportunities and support decision-making.

The case studies cover:

- M&A strategy
- Merger assessment
- Investment feasibility
- Capital allocation
- Business case development
- Investment evaluation
- Strategic investment
- Growth opportunities
- Financial and commercial assessment
- Risk assessment
- Sustainability-linked investment

## Cross-Practice Perspective

Investment decisions rarely operate independently of strategy, finance, risk and sustainability.

Several cases in this practice therefore also have relevance to:

- **Strategy & Transformation** – evaluating strategic rationale, growth opportunities and long-term value creation
- **Finance & Performance** – assessing financial viability, returns and performance implications
- **Risk, Governance & Internal Audit** – considering governance, controls and investment risks
- **Operations Excellence** – assessing operational feasibility and execution requirements
- **ESG & Sustainability** – evaluating sustainability-linked investment and green-growth opportunities

Where a case has significant relevance to another practice, the case remains in its **primary practice folder** and is cross-referenced rather than duplicated.

---

## Approach

The investment and deals perspective generally considers:

**Strategic Rationale → Market / Business Assessment → Commercial Considerations → Financial Analysis → Risk Assessment → Feasibility → Value Creation → Decision Support**

The specific methodology varies by case and is described within the individual case study.

---

## Professional Perspective

Investment decisions require more than attractive financial projections.

A robust investment assessment should consider:

- Strategic fit
- Market opportunity
- Commercial viability
- Financial returns
- Operational requirements
- Execution risks
- Governance
- Sustainability considerations
- Long-term value creation

My approach is therefore to connect:

**Strategy → Commercial Opportunity → Financial Viability → Risk → Execution → Value Creation**
