# School Investment Feasibility Review & Bank Assurance Engagement

**Client:** Confidential Educational Institution (Middle East)  
**Employer:** Morison KSi Qatar  
**Role:** Director – Consulting Services  
**Duration:** 2018–2019

---

## Executive Summary

As Director – Consulting Services at Morison KSi Qatar, I led an assurance engagement to independently review the financial feasibility study prepared by management for the development of a new private school in the Middle East.

The feasibility study had been submitted to a financing bank as part of a loan application. Our role was to provide independent assurance over the reasonableness of management's assumptions, validate the projected financial information, and assess whether the forecasts complied with **International Standard on Assurance Engagements (ISAE) 3400 / SAE 3400 – The Examination of Prospective Financial Information**.

The engagement required the integration of financial analysis, project finance, assurance methodology, educational sector research, and financial modelling. The completed assurance report provided confidence to both the client and the financing bank regarding the reliability of the projected financial information.

---

# Business Context

The client proposed establishing a new private educational institution to address growing demand for quality education within the region.

A detailed feasibility study had already been prepared by management and submitted to the financing bank. Before approving long-term project financing, the bank required an independent assurance review over the assumptions and prospective financial information supporting the investment proposal.

The engagement therefore focused on assessing whether the projected financial performance was reasonable, internally consistent, and supported by appropriate evidence.

---

# Executive Challenge

The financing bank required independent assurance over the following questions:

- Were the financial assumptions reasonable?
- Were projected student enrolments commercially achievable?
- Were projected revenues and operating costs adequately supported?
- Were capital expenditure estimates reasonable?
- Were the projected financial statements prepared using appropriate assumptions?
- Could the prospective financial information be relied upon for financing purposes?

---

# My Role

As Engagement Lead, I was responsible for:

- Planning the assurance engagement.
- Reviewing the feasibility study prepared by management.
- Challenging key commercial and financial assumptions.
- Assessing compliance with assurance standards.
- Reviewing financial projections.
- Preparing the independent assurance report.

# Assurance Methodology

The engagement followed the principles of **ISAE 3400 / SAE 3400 – Examination of Prospective Financial Information**.

---

## Business & Project Review

Reviewed:

- Project objectives.
- School business model.
- Educational services.
- Target student segments.
- Capacity planning.
- Management assumptions.

The objective was to understand the commercial rationale supporting the proposed investment.

---

## Market Assessment

Evaluated:

- Local education market.
- Population growth.
- Demand for private education.
- Competitive landscape.
- Student enrolment assumptions.
- Fee structures.

The analysis assessed whether projected student numbers were commercially achievable.

---

## Financial Assumption Review

Reviewed management assumptions relating to:

- Student admissions.
- Tuition fee growth.
- Staffing levels.
- Payroll costs.
- Operating expenditure.
- Capital expenditure.
- Inflation.
- Working capital requirements.
- Financing assumptions.

Each assumption was evaluated for consistency, completeness and reasonableness.

---

## Financial Model Review

Reviewed and validated:

- Revenue projections.
- Operating cost forecasts.
- Capital expenditure.
- Depreciation assumptions.
- Financing structure.
- Loan repayment schedules.
- Cash flow forecasts.

The review ensured that the financial model was internally consistent and mathematically accurate.

---

## Prospective Financial Statements

Reviewed projected:

- Income Statements.
- Balance Sheets.
- Cash Flow Statements.

The review focused on ensuring that the financial statements appropriately reflected the underlying assumptions adopted by management.

---

## Assurance Evaluation

Assessed whether:

- Assumptions were reasonable.
- Forecasts were internally consistent.
- Financial projections complied with professional assurance standards.
- Prospective financial information could reasonably support the proposed financing application.

---

# Key Findings

## Commercial Assumptions

Management's assumptions regarding student enrolment, pricing strategy and market demand were generally considered reasonable after detailed evaluation and supporting analysis.

---

## Financial Forecasts

Projected revenues, operating costs and financing assumptions were internally consistent and appropriately reflected within the financial model.

---

## Project Viability

The projected financial information demonstrated that the proposed investment appeared commercially viable, subject to management successfully achieving the underlying operating assumptions.

---

## Assurance Conclusion

The review concluded that the prospective financial information had been prepared using reasonable assumptions and was suitable to support the bank financing application in accordance with the applicable assurance standards.
