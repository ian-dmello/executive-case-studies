# Greenhouse Investment Feasibility Study & Financial Evaluation

**Client:** Confidential Agribusiness Company (Middle East)  
**Employer:** Morison KSi Qatar  
**Role:** Director – Consulting Services  
**Duration:** 2018–2019

---

## Executive Summary

As Director – Consulting Services at Morison KSi Qatar, I independently led the preparation of a comprehensive feasibility study for the development of a large-scale commercial greenhouse project in the Middle East.

The study was commissioned to support a bank financing application for the proposed project. The greenhouse formed part of a national food security initiative designed to reduce dependence on imported vegetables by increasing domestic agricultural production using controlled-environment farming.

The engagement required the integration of strategic analysis, market research, technical evaluation, financial modelling, valuation, and investment appraisal to determine the commercial viability of the project and provide an independent recommendation to the financing bank.

The completed feasibility study was accepted by the client and subsequently submitted to the financing bank as part of the loan approval process.

---

# Business Context

Due to the desert climate and limited availability of arable land, conventional agriculture is not commercially viable across much of the Middle East. Governments have therefore invested heavily in controlled-environment agriculture to improve food security and reduce dependence on imported vegetables.

The proposed project involved the construction of twenty commercial hydroponic greenhouses across approximately **99,000 square metres** of government-leased land with a total estimated investment of approximately **QAR 67 million**.

The objective of the engagement was to determine whether the project was commercially viable and whether it justified long-term bank financing.

---

# Executive Challenge

The financing bank required an independent assessment addressing the following questions:

- Was there sufficient market demand for the proposed greenhouse?
- Was the proposed technology commercially viable?
- Would the project generate adequate long-term financial returns?
- Was the proposed financing structure sustainable?
- Should the bank approve the requested project financing?

# Feasibility Assessment Methodology

The study followed a structured investment appraisal methodology covering commercial, operational, technical and financial feasibility.

---

## Business & Sponsor Assessment

Evaluated:

- Project concept.
- Shareholding structure.
- Management capability.
- Existing business operations.
- Industry experience.
- International technical partnerships.

Site visits were conducted to verify land ownership, existing greenhouse operations and overall project readiness.

---

## Market Assessment

Performed comprehensive market research covering:

- Domestic vegetable production.
- Import dependency.
- Export volumes.
- Self-sufficiency levels.
- Future market demand.
- Government food security initiatives.
- Demand-supply gap.
- Customer segmentation.
- Competitive landscape.

Primary pricing research included visits to supermarkets, retailers, hotels and catering businesses to understand prevailing market prices.

The proposed products were positioned as premium-quality greenhouse-grown vegetables targeting higher-value customer segments.

---

## Strategic Assessment

Evaluated:

- Business model.
- Revenue streams.
- Distribution channels.
- Operating processes.
- Competitive positioning.
- SWOT Analysis.

An end-to-end operating process map was developed covering procurement, cultivation, harvesting, grading, packing, distribution, invoicing and collections.

---

## Technical Assessment

Reviewed:

- Hydroponic farming technology.
- Greenhouse construction methodology.
- Equipment specifications.
- Construction quotations.
- Workforce requirements.
- Licensing requirements.
- Crop production planning.
- Construction schedule.
- Expected crop yields.

The assessment concluded that hydroponic technology was particularly suited to Qatar's water-scarce environment due to its efficient use of water and controlled growing conditions.

---

## Financial Assessment

Developed a fully integrated financial model comprising:

- Capital expenditure.
- Pre-operating expenditure.
- Working capital requirements.
- Financing structure.
- Loan repayment schedule.
- Production forecasts.
- Revenue forecasts.
- Operating cost projections.
- Ten-year Income Statements.
- Ten-year Balance Sheets.
- Ten-year Cash Flow Statements.

The model incorporated detailed assumptions relating to production volumes, selling prices, inflation, operating costs, depreciation, financing costs and working capital requirements.

---

## Investment Valuation

Performed comprehensive investment appraisal including:

- Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC).
- Cost of Equity.
- Cost of Debt.
- Net Present Value (NPV).
- Internal Rate of Return (IRR).
- Terminal Value.
- Project Payback Period.

The valuation demonstrated that the project generated positive long-term returns and was financially viable.

---

# Key Recommendations

## Commercial Strategy

Recommended positioning the greenhouse as a supplier of premium-quality produce while recognising the long-term impact of increasing domestic competition on pricing.

Following discussions with the client, long-term selling price assumptions were revised from premium organic pricing to conventional market pricing to reflect expected competitive conditions as additional greenhouse projects entered the market.

---

## Food Security

Demonstrated that the project would contribute towards reducing import dependency and supporting the government's long-term food security objectives.

---

## Technical Design

Validated hydroponic farming as the preferred production methodology due to its efficient use of water, improved crop yields and suitability for desert climates.

---

## Investment Decision

Concluded that the project demonstrated sufficient commercial and financial viability to justify long-term bank financing.
