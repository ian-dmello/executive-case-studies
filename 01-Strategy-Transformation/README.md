# Strategy & Transformation

## Overview

This practice brings together case studies involving **strategy development, business transformation, growth, performance improvement, value creation and strategic reviews**.

The case studies demonstrate experience in translating complex business issues into structured strategic initiatives, with consideration of governance, financial performance, operational effectiveness, risk and long-term value creation.

---

## Areas of Experience

The case studies cover:

- Corporate and business strategy
- Business transformation
- Strategic business reviews
- Growth strategy
- Performance improvement
- Turnaround and value creation
- Enterprise transformation
- Real estate strategy and transformation
- Telecom strategy and value creation
- SME performance transformation
- M&A strategy

## Cross-Practice Perspective

Strategy and transformation rarely operate in isolation.

Several cases in this practice also involve:

- **Risk & Governance** – strengthening governance structures, controls and accountability
- **Finance & Performance** – improving financial performance and value creation
- **Operations Excellence** – improving processes, operating models and execution
- **Deals & Investment** – assessing strategic transactions and investment opportunities

Where a case has significant relevance to another practice, the case remains in its **primary practice folder** and is cross-referenced rather than duplicated.

---

## Approach

Across these case studies, the strategic perspective generally considers:

**Business Context → Current-State Assessment → Key Issues → Strategic Options → Transformation Priorities → Implementation Considerations → Value Creation**

The specific methodology varies by case and is described within the individual case study.

---

## Professional Perspective

My experience across strategy and transformation has reinforced the importance of connecting strategy with execution.

A strategy is only valuable when it can be translated into:

- Clear priorities
- Accountable ownership
- Measurable outcomes
- Appropriate governance
- Sustainable financial and operational improvements

The case studies therefore look beyond strategy formulation to consider **how strategic objectives can translate into practical business outcomes**.
