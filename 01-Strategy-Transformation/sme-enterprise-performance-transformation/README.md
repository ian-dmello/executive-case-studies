# Enterprise Performance Transformation Review for a Leading Construction Materials Manufacturer

**Client:** SMEET W.L.L., Qatar  
**Employer:** Protiviti Middle East  
**Role:** Project Manager – Enterprise Performance & Internal Audit Review  
**Duration:** 2015

---

# Executive Summary

As Project Manager from Protiviti Middle East, I led an enterprise-wide performance, governance and operational review of **SMEET W.L.L.**, one of Qatar's leading manufacturers of ready-mix concrete, precast concrete products and concrete blocks.

The engagement extended significantly beyond a traditional internal audit. It evaluated the company's financial performance, manufacturing efficiency, sales strategy, procurement practices, working capital management, corporate governance, enterprise risk management, information technology, project costing and operational controls.

The review identified **44 audit observations**, including **21 High Risk** and **16 Moderate Risk** observations, together with practical recommendations designed to improve profitability, governance, operational efficiency and long-term sustainability.

---

# Company Background

SMEET W.L.L. is one of Qatar's leading manufacturers of ready-mix concrete, precast concrete products and concrete blocks, supplying major infrastructure, commercial and residential developments across the country.

**At the time of this engagement (2015)**, SMEET operated as a strategic associate of **Barwa Real Estate Company (Q.P.S.C.)**, with ownership shared between **Qatari Diar**, **Barwa Real Estate**, and **The First Investor**.

The company operated multiple production facilities and employed approximately 2,000 personnel, making it one of Qatar's largest construction materials manufacturers.

**Current Status:** Since this engagement, SMEET's ownership has changed and the company is now part of **Dynam Holding**. This case study reflects the ownership structure, operating environment and business challenges that existed during the 2015 engagement.

# Business Context

The construction materials industry in Qatar was experiencing rapid expansion due to major infrastructure investments and real estate developments.

Despite operating in a high-growth market, increasing competition, pricing pressure, rising operating costs and significant capital investments required management to continuously improve operational efficiency and strengthen governance.

Protiviti Middle East was engaged to perform an enterprise-wide review covering operational, financial and governance processes, with the objective of identifying opportunities to improve profitability, strengthen internal controls and support long-term business performance.
