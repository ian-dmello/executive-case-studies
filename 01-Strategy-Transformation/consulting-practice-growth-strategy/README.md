# Consulting Practice Growth Strategy

**Client:** Morison KSi Qatar (Internal Strategic Initiative)  
**Employer:** Morison KSi Qatar  
**Role:** Director – Consulting Services  
**Duration:** 2018–2019

---

## Executive Summary

Upon joining Morison KSi Qatar as Director – Consulting Services, I was tasked with expanding the firm's consulting practice, which had experienced stagnant growth. The Managing Director challenged the practice to match or exceed the previous year's consulting revenue.

Rather than pursuing isolated sales opportunities, I adopted a structured consulting approach to develop a sustainable growth strategy. The engagement involved analysing the external market, assessing the firm's competitive position, identifying new service offerings, strengthening proposal quality, and implementing business development initiatives.

Working closely with the Practice Partner, I led the implementation of the growth strategy. As a result, the consulting practice exceeded the previous year's revenue target by **April 2019**, several months before the financial year-end.

---

# Business Context

Morison KSi Qatar is part of a leading international association of accounting and consulting firms.

Although the audit practice remained well established, the consulting division had experienced limited growth and relied heavily on traditional service offerings. The firm sought to expand its market presence, diversify consulting services, and improve proposal conversion rates within Qatar's evolving business environment.

---

# Executive Challenge

The Managing Director required a practical strategy to:

- Increase consulting revenue.
- Improve proposal conversion rates.
- Expand the firm's client portfolio.
- Introduce new advisory services.
- Strengthen market positioning.
- Develop sustainable long-term business growth.

---

# My Role

As Director – Consulting Services, I led the strategic review and business development initiative.

My responsibilities included:

- Conducting market and competitive analysis.
- Reviewing the consulting practice's performance.
- Identifying new market opportunities.
- Developing service expansion strategies.

# Strategic Review Methodology

The engagement adopted a structured strategy consulting methodology.

## External Environment Analysis

Conducted a comprehensive **PESTLE Analysis** evaluating:

- Political developments.
- Economic conditions.
- Regulatory environment.
- Social trends.
- Technological developments.
- Legal considerations.

Particular attention was given to the regional geopolitical environment, including the Gulf blockade and its impact on demand for locally based consulting firms.

---

## Internal Strategic Assessment

Performed a **SWOT Analysis** of the consulting practice covering:

- Existing capabilities.
- Market reputation.
- Competitive advantages.
- Service gaps.
- Growth opportunities.
- Operational constraints.

The review identified significant opportunities to expand advisory services beyond traditional consulting assignments.

---

## Industry & Competitive Analysis

Applied **Porter's Five Forces Framework** to evaluate:

- Competitive rivalry.
- New market entrants.
- Client bargaining power.
- Supplier influence.
- Substitute advisory services.

Benchmarking against competing consulting firms helped identify service offerings and industries where the firm had limited market penetration.

---

## Business Performance Review

Analysed internal management information, including:

- Proposal volumes.
- Proposal conversion rates.
- Revenue trends.
- Service line performance.
- Existing client portfolio.
- Win-loss analysis.

The review highlighted opportunities to improve both business development effectiveness and proposal quality.

---

# Key Strategic Initiatives

## Market Expansion

Identified major government entities, family-owned business groups, financial institutions, and large private sector organisations where the firm had limited consulting presence.

Developed targeted client acquisition strategies to expand the firm's consulting portfolio.

---

## Service Portfolio Expansion

Recommended introducing several higher-value advisory services, including:

- Enterprise Risk Management (ERM)
- Corporate Governance
- Strategy Consulting
- Business Process Transformation
- IT Advisory
- Credit Risk Management
- IFRS Implementation
- In-Country Value (Tawteen) Advisory

These services aligned with emerging market demand and the firm's existing capabilities.

---

## Proposal Transformation

Reviewed proposal quality and identified significant opportunities for improvement.

Recommended replacing standard Word-based proposals with professionally designed PowerPoint presentations wherever permitted by procurement requirements.

The revised approach improved presentation quality, strengthened the firm's value proposition, and enhanced differentiation during competitive bidding.

---

## Tender Intelligence

Researched government procurement portals and commercial tender platforms to improve visibility of consulting opportunities.

Developed a structured process for monitoring strategic tenders relevant to the firm's advisory practice.

---

## Marketing & Client Engagement

Designed a comprehensive consulting capability brochure showcasing:

- Firm profile.
- Service offerings.
- Organisation structure.
- Representative engagements.
- Client portfolio.
- Global capabilities through the Morison KSi network.

The material was used during executive meetings and targeted marketing campaigns directed at senior executives within major organisations across Qatar.

---

# Results Delivered

- Developed a structured business growth strategy for the consulting practice.
- Expanded the firm's consulting service portfolio.
- Improved proposal quality and competitive positioning.
- Enhanced business development processes.
- Increased engagement with strategic clients.
- Strengthened the firm's market visibility.
- The consulting practice exceeded the previous year's confirmed consulting revenue by **April 2019**, well before the financial year-end.
