# Strategic Business Review & Transformation Roadmap

**Client:** Confidential Family-Owned Diversified Business Group  
**Employer:** Morison KSi Qatar  
**Role:** Director – Consulting Services  
**Duration:** 2018–2019

---

## Executive Summary

As Director – Consulting Services at Morison KSi Qatar, I secured a consulting engagement to conduct a comprehensive strategic business review of a diversified family-owned business group.

The Group's businesses included commercial real estate leasing, travel services, airline ticketing, and related investments. Revenue growth had stagnated, and profitability was heavily dependent on a single business unit operating as the exclusive General Sales Agent (GSA) for an international airline.

The Deputy Chairman engaged our firm to identify the underlying causes of the Group's stagnant growth and provide practical recommendations to improve business performance, strengthen governance, and enhance long-term profitability. In addition to the strategic business review, I was also asked to conduct an independent organisational and human capital assessment covering employee performance, organisational structure, and leadership effectiveness.

The engagement resulted in two executive reports that provided a transformation roadmap for the Group. The Deputy Chairman commended the quality and practicality of the recommendations, noting that they exceeded the standard of previous consulting engagements.

---

# Business Context

The Group operated a diversified portfolio of businesses across:

- Commercial Real Estate
- Property Leasing
- Travel & Tourism
- Airline Ticketing
- Investment Activities

Despite operating across multiple sectors, the Group remained highly dependent on a single airline partnership for profitability. Executive management sought an independent assessment of business performance together with recommendations to improve growth, governance, and organisational effectiveness.

---

# Executive Challenge

Executive management required an independent review to answer the following strategic questions:

- Why had business growth stagnated?
- How could revenue be diversified?
- Which operational improvements would increase profitability?
- What organisational changes were required?
- How could governance and performance management be strengthened?

# Key Strategic Recommendations

## Revenue Diversification

Recommended reducing dependence on the existing airline partnership by expanding relationships with additional airlines and developing alternative revenue streams.

This recommendation proved particularly significant as the airline subsequently ceased operations, validating the importance of revenue diversification.

---

## Property Optimisation

Identified vacant commercial properties generating an estimated opportunity cost of approximately **QAR 1.4 million annually**.

Recommended initiatives to improve occupancy and increase recurring rental income.

---

## Financial Reporting

Recommended reassessment of:

- Investment property valuations.
- IFRS 9 expected credit loss provisions.
- Contingent liabilities.
- Credit risk management.

These recommendations strengthened financial reporting and risk management.

---

## Business Expansion

Recommended expanding the Group's civil contracting activities by leveraging existing in-house engineering resources to generate new revenue streams from external customers.

---

## Customer Strategy

Recommended increasing the proportion of corporate travel clients to create more stable and predictable recurring revenue.

---

## Organisational Transformation

Recommended:

- Revised organisation structure.
- Improved KPIs.
- Enhanced performance management.
- Better alignment between employee responsibilities and strategic objectives.
- Strengthened accountability across management.
