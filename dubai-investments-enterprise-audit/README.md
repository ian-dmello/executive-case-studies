# Dubai Investments PJSC (UAE)
## Enterprise Internal Audit Across a Diversified Conglomerate

**Role:** Assistant Manager – Internal Audit  
**Duration:** June 2012 – May 2014

---

## Executive Summary

As an **Assistant Manager** within the Group Internal Audit function at **Dubai Investments PJSC**, I led and executed risk-based internal audits across a diversified portfolio of manufacturing, industrial, real estate, engineering, pharmaceutical, dairy, and trading businesses.

While reporting through the audit management structure, I regularly **presented and discussed audit reports, key observations, and recommendations directly with the Group Internal Audit Manager (GIAM)** during the audit review process.

Over a two-year period, I successfully completed **approximately 22 internal audit engagements** across **47 subsidiaries**, working within a **5–8 member audit team** and consistently delivering high-quality audit outcomes within established timelines.

---

## Business Context

Dubai Investments PJSC is one of the UAE's leading diversified investment groups with operations spanning manufacturing, industrial services, engineering, real estate, pharmaceuticals, dairy production, and trading.

Each subsidiary presented unique operational, financial, and regulatory risks, requiring a consistent risk-based audit methodology while adapting to different business models and industry-specific processes.

---

## My Responsibilities

- Led risk-based internal audit engagements across multiple subsidiaries within the Dubai Investments Group.
- Presented and discussed audit reports, key findings, and recommendations directly with the **Group Internal Audit Manager (GIAM)** during the audit review process.
- Executed detailed reviews of high-risk operational, financial, and compliance processes.
- Reviewed working papers prepared by audit team members to ensure quality, completeness, and consistency.
- Finalized audit reports with practical, risk-based recommendations for management.
- Mentored junior auditors by providing technical guidance and constructive feedback.
- Monitored implementation of agreed management action plans and validated corrective actions.

## Key Achievements

### Fraud Detection and Governance

- Identified an **irregular vendor payment of approximately AED 0.5 million** made through stake cheques, uncovering a significant conflict of interest involving the General Manager, who subsequently joined the vendor organization.
- Uncovered a **credit note fraud of approximately AED 1 million**, exposing weaknesses in authorization controls, segregation of duties, and management oversight.

### Operational Excellence

- Identified a significant production measurement discrepancy at **Marmum Dairy Farm** by detecting unresolved calibration issues affecting two flow meters on the same production pipeline, improving the reliability of production and inventory reporting.
