## Cross-Practice Perspective

Sustainability considerations increasingly form part of broader business and investment decisions.

Relevant connections include:

- **Deals & Investment** – evaluating sustainable investment opportunities and financial viability
- **Strategy & Transformation** – incorporating sustainability into strategic decision-making
- **Finance & Performance** – assessing the financial implications of sustainability initiatives
- **Operations Excellence** – considering resource efficiency and sustainable operating models
- **Risk & Governance** – considering environmental and emerging business risks

The relevant case studies therefore remain in their **primary practice folders**, with this section providing an additional ESG-oriented route for the reader.

---

## Approach

The sustainability perspective generally considers:

**Strategic Objective → Sustainability Opportunity → Commercial Feasibility → Financial Assessment → Risk → Implementation → Long-Term Value**

The specific methodology varies by case and is described within the individual case study.

---

## Professional Perspective

Sustainability should increasingly be considered as part of mainstream business decision-making rather than as a standalone reporting exercise.

From a business and investment perspective, relevant questions include:

- Is the opportunity commercially viable?
- What are the financial implications?
- What risks are involved?
- How does sustainability affect long-term value?
- What operational changes are required?
- How can sustainability objectives be incorporated into implementation?

My perspective is therefore to connect:

**Sustainability → Strategy → Investment → Risk → Financial Viability → Long-Term Value**
