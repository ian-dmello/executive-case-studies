# Forensics & Investigations

## Overview

This practice brings together case studies involving **forensic investigations, whistleblower matters, fraud risk, investigative reviews and the analysis of control failures**.

The focus is on understanding the circumstances surrounding an incident, identifying underlying risks and control weaknesses, and translating investigative findings into practical management actions.

## Approach

The investigative perspective generally considers:

**Trigger / Allegation → Scope Definition → Evidence Gathering → Analysis → Findings → Root Cause → Risk Assessment → Management Recommendations**

The specific methodology varies by case and is described within the individual case study.

---

## Professional Perspective

Forensic investigations require a combination of **professional scepticism, analytical thinking, evidence assessment and sound judgement**.

The objective is not simply to identify what went wrong, but to understand:

- What happened
- Why it happened
- Who or what was affected
- Which controls failed
- Whether the issue may be systemic
- What risks remain
- What management should do next

My approach is therefore to connect:

**Evidence → Facts → Analysis → Root Cause → Risk → Remediation**
