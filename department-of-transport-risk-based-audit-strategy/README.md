# Department of Transport (DoT) & TransAD (Abu Dhabi)

## Enterprise Risk Assessment and Three-Year Risk-Based Internal Audit Strategy

**Client:** Department of Transport (DoT) & TransAD, Abu Dhabi  
**Employer:** Protiviti Middle East  
**Role:** Assistant Manager (Audit Lead)  
**Duration:** August 2010 – May 2012

---

## Executive Summary

As **Audit Lead** with Protiviti Middle East, I led the development of comprehensive **three-year risk-based internal audit plans** for the **Department of Transport (DoT)** and **TransAD**, Abu Dhabi's public transport authority responsible for transportation planning, regulation, and taxi services.

Leading a team of **2–3 audit analysts**, I performed an enterprise-wide risk assessment covering **40 divisions within DoT** and **16 divisions within TransAD**. The engagement combined Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) principles with COSO-based internal control evaluation to establish a structured, risk-driven audit universe.

The resulting three-year internal audit plans prioritized audit coverage based on residual risk, management priorities, and organizational risk exposure. Both audit plans were formally approved by the Audit Committee and formed the basis for subsequent internal audit engagements.

Following approval, I led several operational, procurement, governance, and compliance audits across both organizations, identifying significant governance, procurement, and contract management deficiencies.

---

## Business Context

The Department of Transport (DoT) was responsible for planning, regulating, and overseeing Abu Dhabi's transportation infrastructure and services. TransAD was responsible for regulating and managing the Emirate's taxi services and associated transportation operations.

Given the scale of public expenditure, infrastructure projects, procurement activities, and regulatory responsibilities, management required a structured, enterprise-wide, risk-based internal audit program that would:

- Align audit coverage with organizational risks.
- Optimize deployment of internal audit resources.
- Strengthen governance and internal controls.
- Provide independent assurance to the Audit Committee.
- Establish a sustainable three-year audit strategy.

## Risk-Based Audit Planning Methodology

The engagement followed a structured methodology aligned with the **COSO Enterprise Risk Management Framework** and **The Institute of Internal Auditors' Risk-Based Internal Audit Planning methodology**.

### Phase 1 – Audit Universe Development

- Identified all auditable entities, departments, and business processes.
- Defined the enterprise audit universe.
- Established audit boundaries and ownership.

---

### Phase 2 – Enterprise Risk Assessment

Conducted structured interviews and workshops with Heads of Departments and senior management to identify:

- Strategic risks
- Operational risks
- Financial risks
- Regulatory risks
- Project risks
- Reputational risks

Risks were identified through document reviews, preliminary analysis, historical audit findings, and management discussions.

---

### Phase 3 – Risk & Control Assessment

For each of the **56 divisions**:

- Documented key business processes.
- Prepared comprehensive Risk & Control Matrices (RCMs).
- Identified key controls.
- Evaluated control design.
- Tested selected controls.
- Assessed control effectiveness.

---

### Phase 4 – Residual Risk Assessment

Residual risk was determined by evaluating:

- Inherent risk
- Existing control effectiveness
- Control gaps
- Remaining exposure after mitigation

Each department received an overall residual risk rating.

---

### Phase 5 – Enterprise Risk Heat Maps

Residual risks were consolidated into enterprise-wide heat maps to:

- Prioritize audit resources.
- Determine audit periodicity.
- Sequence audit engagements.
- Allocate audit effort over the three-year audit cycle.

---

### Phase 6 – Three-Year Audit Strategy

Based on residual risk:

- High-risk functions were scheduled for annual audits.
- Medium-risk functions were incorporated into the rotational audit cycle.
- Lower-risk areas were scheduled periodically while maintaining overall assurance coverage.

The completed audit plans were reviewed with senior management and formally approved by the Audit Committee.

## Key Achievements

### Three-Year Risk-Based Audit Strategy

- Developed comprehensive three-year risk-based internal audit plans for **Department of Transport (40 divisions)** and **TransAD (16 divisions)**.
- Conducted enterprise-wide risk assessments covering **56 business divisions**.
- Designed Risk & Control Matrices (RCMs) for every division.
- Developed residual risk assessments and enterprise heat maps to prioritize audit coverage.
- Successfully obtained **Audit Committee approval** for both three-year audit strategies.

### Procurement Governance

Identified procurement contracts awarded to relatives of a senior executive, exposing significant conflicts of interest and weaknesses in procurement governance, vendor due diligence, and ethical oversight.

### Financial Controls

Identified payments amounting to **millions of AED** where no evidence existed that contracted services had been performed, highlighting deficiencies in contract administration, invoice validation, and payment authorization.

### Contractor Performance Management

Identified payments made to a road construction contractor despite documented engineering reports highlighting significant road quality deficiencies, resulting in recommendations to strengthen contractor performance evaluation, quality assurance, and contract governance.
