# Finance & Performance

## Overview

This practice brings together case studies focused on **financial performance, financial reporting, business performance improvement, management information and value creation**.

The case studies demonstrate how financial analysis can be combined with business, operational, governance and strategic perspectives to identify performance issues and support management decision-making.

---

## Areas of Experience

The case studies cover:

- Financial performance review
- Financial reporting
- Performance improvement
- Management reporting
- Business turnaround
- Value creation
- Financial analysis
- Performance monitoring
- Management information
- Financial and operational decision support

## Cross-Practice Perspective

Financial performance is closely connected with broader business priorities.

Several case studies across this portfolio therefore have relevance to:

- **Strategy & Transformation** – translating financial performance into strategic priorities and value-creation initiatives
- **Risk & Governance** – strengthening financial controls, reporting governance and accountability
- **Operations Excellence** – improving processes that drive financial performance
- **Deals & Investment** – assessing financial implications of investments and strategic transactions

Where a case has significant relevance to another practice, the case remains in its **primary practice folder** and is cross-referenced rather than duplicated.

---

## Approach

The financial and performance perspective generally considers:

**Business Context → Financial Analysis → Performance Drivers → Key Issues → Improvement Opportunities → Management Actions → Performance Monitoring**

The specific methodology varies by case and is described within the individual case study.

---

## Professional Perspective

Financial information is most valuable when it helps management understand **what is happening, why it is happening and what should be done next**.

My approach therefore goes beyond historical reporting to consider:

- Performance drivers
- Variances and trends
- Business and operational causes
- Financial implications
- Risks and control considerations
- Improvement opportunities
- Management actions
- Sustainable value creation

The objective is to connect:

**Financial Information → Business Insight → Management Action → Performance Improvement**
