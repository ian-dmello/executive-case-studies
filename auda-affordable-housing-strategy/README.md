# Affordable Housing Sustainability & Cost Optimization Strategy

**Client:** Ahmedabad Urban Development Authority (AUDA)  
**Institution:** Indian Institute of Management Ahmedabad (IIMA)  
**Role:** MBA Participant – Public Sector Consulting Initiative  
**Duration:** 2017–2018

---

## Executive Summary

As part of an extracurricular consulting initiative during the MBA programme at the Indian Institute of Management Ahmedabad (IIMA), our team evaluated a government-funded affordable housing project developed by the Ahmedabad Urban Development Authority (AUDA).

The housing project, funded through the Prime Minister's Development Fund, was designed to provide affordable homes for economically weaker sections of society. The engagement focused on improving the long-term financial sustainability of the housing development while minimising maintenance charges for residents.

The completed report combined operational analysis, cost optimisation, revenue generation, sustainability initiatives and social infrastructure recommendations. The report was presented to the Indian Administrative Service (IAS) officer responsible for the project and received positive feedback.

---

# Business Context

The affordable housing project was developed to provide subsidised housing for low-income families.

Although the capital cost of construction was government funded, long-term maintenance expenses would ultimately be borne by residents through monthly maintenance charges.

The objective of the engagement was to identify practical initiatives that would reduce operating costs, generate recurring revenue and improve the long-term sustainability of the community.

# Consulting Methodology

## Operational Review

Reviewed:

- Maintenance operations.
- Staffing levels.
- Building services.
- Facilities management.

The assessment identified opportunities to optimise recurring maintenance expenditure.

---

## Revenue Enhancement Assessment

Evaluated opportunities including:

- Commercial utilisation of vacant spaces.
- Telecommunications tower rentals.
- Additional rental income.
- Alternative revenue streams.

---

## Sustainability Assessment

Reviewed:

- Solar power installation.
- Water treatment facilities.
- Environmental benefits.
- Long-term operating costs.

A cost-benefit analysis was performed to assess financial viability.

---

## Community Infrastructure Assessment

Evaluated:

- Healthcare facilities.
- Nursery and childcare.
- Street lighting.
- Shopping facilities.
- Community safety.
- Environmental conditions.

The objective was to improve both financial sustainability and resident well-being.

---

# Key Recommendations

## Cost Optimisation

Recommended eliminating unnecessary maintenance staffing positions where services were not being effectively utilised, thereby reducing recurring maintenance charges paid by residents.

---

## CSR Funding

Recommended that qualifying listed companies support maintenance expenditure through Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) initiatives permitted under the Companies Act, thereby reducing the financial burden on low-income residents.

---

## Revenue Generation

Recommended:

- Leasing unused ground-floor space for commercial activities.
- Installing telecommunications towers to generate recurring rental income.
- Creating additional commercial facilities within the community.

---

## Community Development

Recommended:

- Development of an affordable supermarket.
- Improved healthcare access.
- Enhanced street lighting.
- Better community infrastructure.

---

## Environmental Improvements

Recommended relocation of a nearby dairy facility due to environmental concerns together with broader sustainability initiatives supporting improved living conditions.

---

# Results Delivered

- Developed recommendations to reduce long-term maintenance costs.
- Identified recurring revenue generation opportunities.
- Completed sustainability and cost-benefit assessments.
- Proposed improvements to community infrastructure.
- Supported long-term financial sustainability of the housing project.
- Presented recommendations to the IAS officer responsible for the project.
- Received positive feedback regarding the quality and practicality of the recommendations.
