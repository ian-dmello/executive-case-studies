**Client:** Alfardan Group, Qatar  
**Employer:** Protiviti Middle East  
**Role:** Interim Group Internal Audit Manager (Secondment)  
**Duration:** 2015 (Approximately 5 Months)

# Executive Summary

Following the successful completion of the Barwa Real Estate co-sourcing engagement, I was seconded by Protiviti Middle East to **Alfardan Group** as the **Interim Group Internal Audit Manager**.

The Internal Audit function required immediate operational stabilisation following leadership changes and a growing backlog of audit assignments. The objective of the engagement was to restore delivery momentum, strengthen governance, improve audit quality and ensure successful execution of the Group's Internal Audit Plan while supporting the recruitment and transition to a permanent Group Internal Audit Manager.

During approximately five months leading the Internal Audit function, I transformed the department into a structured, high-performing assurance function by introducing disciplined audit planning, strengthening quality assurance, enhancing stakeholder communication and accelerating audit delivery.

The engagement resulted in:

- Completion of **18 internal audit engagements**
- Finalisation and issuance of **16 audit reports**
- Execution of **4 confidential special assignments**
- Regular reporting to the Audit Committee
- Establishment of the Q3 Internal Audit Programme
- Strengthening of audit methodology, governance and quality assurance processes
- Coaching and supervision of the Internal Audit team

The engagement demonstrated the ability to rapidly stabilise and lead a Group Internal Audit function while delivering measurable business outcomes across a diversified conglomerate operating multiple businesses throughout Qatar. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

# Risk-Based Internal Audit Planning

The Internal Audit Department adopted a structured risk-based audit methodology to ensure that audit resources were directed towards the areas presenting the greatest business risk.

Audit planning considered:

- Strategic business objectives.
- Financial exposure.
- Operational complexity.
- Regulatory requirements.
- Prior audit observations.
- Fraud risk.
- Internal control maturity.
- Changes in business operations.

The approach ensured that audit coverage aligned with the Group's overall risk profile while providing the Audit Committee with assurance over the most significant risks affecting the organisation.

# Enterprise Audit Methodology

Each audit assignment followed a structured methodology aligned with the International Standards for the Professional Practice of Internal Auditing (IIA Standards).

The methodology consisted of:

## Planning

- Understanding business objectives.
- Reviewing previous audit reports.
- Identifying key risks.
- Preparing detailed audit programmes.
- Defining audit scope.

---

## Process Understanding

The audit team developed a comprehensive understanding of business operations through:

- Process walkthroughs.
- Executive interviews.
- Policy reviews.
- Delegation of Authority reviews.
- Review of organisational structures.
- Identification of key controls.

---

## Risk & Control Assessment

Each business process was evaluated to determine:

- Inherent risks.
- Existing preventive controls.
- Detective controls.
- Control effectiveness.
- Residual risks.
- Governance weaknesses.

Root causes of control deficiencies were identified to ensure recommendations addressed underlying business issues rather than isolated observations.

---

## Audit Execution

Audit procedures included:

- Financial analysis.
- Operational reviews.
- Data validation.
- Interviews with management.
- Review of supporting documentation.

Audit evidence was evaluated objectively to ensure conclusions were fully supported before reporting observations.
