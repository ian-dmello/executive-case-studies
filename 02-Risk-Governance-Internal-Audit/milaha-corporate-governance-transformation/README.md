# Enterprise Corporate Governance Transformation for Qatar Navigation (Milaha)

**Client:** Qatar Navigation Q.P.S.C. (Milaha)  
**Period:** 2014–2015  
**Organization:** Protiviti Qatar  
**Role:** Manager – Internal Audit & Governance Advisory  

---

# Executive Summary

Soon after joining Protiviti Qatar, I was assigned one of the firm's most prestigious governance advisory engagements for Qatar Navigation (Milaha), one of Qatar's largest publicly listed companies.

The Corporate Governance Gap Assessment had fallen behind schedule and required substantial restructuring before it could be presented to senior leadership. The engagement Director entrusted me with reviewing the work completed, strengthening the governance assessment, redesigning the report structure and developing a practical governance transformation roadmap.

The assignment involved evaluating Milaha's Corporate Governance Framework against leading international governance standards and developing a comprehensive roadmap to strengthen governance maturity across the organisation.

Following completion of the review and incorporation of management feedback, the final report was presented to the Chairman and Executive Management.

---

# Business Context

Milaha is one of Qatar's oldest and largest publicly listed companies, with diversified operations across maritime transport, logistics, offshore services, shipping, trading, capital investments and real estate.

As a listed entity on the Qatar Stock Exchange, the company is required to maintain strong corporate governance practices and comply with the Qatar Financial Markets Authority (QFMA) Corporate Governance Code.

The engagement was undertaken to evaluate the maturity of Milaha's governance framework, identify improvement opportunities and benchmark existing practices against internationally recognised governance standards.

# Key Governance Observations

The review identified governance improvement opportunities across five major governance pillars.

## 1. Board Governance

Recommendations included:

- Formal Board Independence Framework
- Annual Board Evaluations
- Board Performance KPIs
- Board Training Programme
- Comprehensive Board Charter
- Annual Board Agenda
- Enhanced Board Secretary responsibilities

---

## 2. Board Committee Governance

Recommendations included:

- Formal reporting by Board Committees
- Comprehensive Committee Charters
- Nomination Committee establishment
- Enhanced Audit Committee governance
- Strengthened Remuneration Committee responsibilities

---

## 3. Management Governance

Recommendations included:

- Board-approved Organisation Structure
- Enhanced Delegation of Authority
- Management Committee Charters
- Powers of Attorney governance
