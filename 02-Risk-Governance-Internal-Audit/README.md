# Risk, Governance & Internal Audit

## Overview

This practice brings together case studies covering **internal audit, enterprise risk management, corporate governance, SOX, assurance, risk-based auditing and governance transformation**.

The case studies demonstrate experience in strengthening governance frameworks, improving risk visibility, enhancing internal audit effectiveness and supporting management through independent assurance and practical recommendations.

## Cross-Practice Perspective

Risk, governance and internal audit frequently intersect with broader business priorities.

Several cases in this practice also have relevance to:

- **Strategy & Transformation** – aligning governance and risk frameworks with strategic objectives
- **Finance & Performance** – strengthening financial controls, reporting and performance oversight
- **Operations Excellence** – improving processes, controls and operating effectiveness
- **Forensics & Investigations** – responding to fraud risks, whistleblower matters and special investigations
- **Deals & Investment** – governance and assurance over major investments and capital projects

Where a case has significant relevance to another practice, the case remains in its **primary practice folder** and is cross-referenced rather than duplicated.

## Approach

Across these case studies, the risk and governance perspective generally considers:

**Business Context → Risk Assessment → Governance & Control Framework → Audit / Assurance Approach → Findings → Management Recommendations → Monitoring & Improvement**

The specific methodology varies by case and is described within the individual case study.

---

## Professional Perspective

Effective internal audit and governance functions should go beyond identifying control gaps.

They should help management understand:

- What matters most
- Where significant risks exist
- Whether controls are operating effectively
- What requires management attention
- How risks can be reduced
- How governance can support business objectives

My approach therefore focuses on connecting:

**Risk → Controls → Assurance → Management Insight → Business Value**
