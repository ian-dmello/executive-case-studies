# Procurement Governance & Supply Chain Assurance

As part of the project audit, I performed a detailed review of procurement planning, supplier qualification and long-lead material procurement for the Qatar Foundation Nursery Project.

The objective was to evaluate whether procurement activities were adequately supporting construction progress while complying with ASTAD's procurement governance framework.

The review covered:

- Procurement planning processes.
- Long-lead material procurement.
- Supplier pre-qualification.
- Preferred Vendor List (PVL) compliance.
- Procurement approvals.
- Coordination between contractor and consultant.
- Procurement monitoring and reporting.

---

## Key Observations

The audit identified several governance weaknesses that had the potential to impact project delivery.

### Delay in Procurement of Critical Materials

The review identified significant delays in procuring long-lead construction materials.

Examples included:

- Procurement requests that had not yet been initiated.
- Approved procurement requests dating back to earlier years where procurement had still not been completed.
- Twenty-nine major procurement requests submitted during 2014 that remained pending receipt.

### Weaknesses in Vendor Governance

A sample review of procurement transactions identified multiple vendors that were not included within ASTAD's Preferred Vendor List (PVL).

The observations indicated weaknesses in procurement governance and supplier qualification processes.

The audit concluded that procurement from non-approved vendors increased the risk of:

- Quality issues.
- Procurement delays.
- Higher procurement costs.
- Reduced consistency in supplier evaluation.
- Increased contractual risk.

## Business Impact

The procurement observations highlighted several enterprise risks including:

- Delay in project completion.
- Increased construction costs.
- Procurement inefficiencies.
- Reduced contractor accountability.
- Increased supply chain risk.
- Potential quality concerns relating to construction materials.
- Weak compliance with established procurement governance policies.

---

## Recommendations

Recommendations focused on strengthening procurement governance by:

- Intensifying follow-up between the consultant and contractor for outstanding procurement requests.
- Improving monitoring of long-lead procurement activities.
- Restricting procurement to vendors approved under ASTAD's Preferred Vendor List.
- Strengthening procurement planning and supplier governance.
- Improving management oversight of procurement progress throughout the project lifecycle.

These recommendations were discussed with the Senior Landscape Engineer, Senior Project Manager and Resident Engineer before incorporation into the final audit report. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}
