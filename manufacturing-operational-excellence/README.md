# Executive Summary

I led a governance and operational improvement engagement for the Indian subsidiary of a German multinational manufacturing company producing industrial heat exchange solutions.

The engagement was undertaken during a challenging period following significant control failures within the sales function. Executive management sought an independent review to restore confidence in the internal audit programme, strengthen governance and identify opportunities to improve operational performance.

As Project Manager, I coordinated the engagement remotely from another city, managing delivery, reviewing working papers and ensuring consistent quality across multiple audit workstreams. I served as the primary interface with senior management throughout the engagement, presenting findings directly to the Chief Financial Officer and supporting discussions leading to final acceptance of the reports.

The assignment extended beyond traditional assurance activities by identifying operational inefficiencies, strengthening governance and providing practical recommendations to improve manufacturing performance and financial oversight.

---

# Business Context

The company manufactured industrial heat exchange equipment for domestic and international markets through its Indian production facility.

At the time of the engagement, management was addressing the aftermath of significant control failures while simultaneously seeking to improve profitability, operational efficiency and governance across key business functions.

The objective was to restore confidence in the internal audit process and provide management with actionable recommendations that supported operational stability and long-term business performance.

# Executive Reflection

This engagement demonstrated that effective internal audit can play a critical role in restoring organisational confidence following control failures. By focusing on operational improvement, evidence-based reporting and constructive engagement with management, the audit function became a catalyst for performance improvement rather than simply a compliance exercise.

If undertaking this engagement today, I would enhance the approach through predictive maintenance analytics, process mining, AI-assisted anomaly detection and executive dashboards to provide management with real-time visibility over operational performance, inventory management and manufacturing efficiency.
